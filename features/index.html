Everything Zoopy does, and which plan does it

Zoopy watches Facebook Marketplace for you and sends a push when a new listing matches your hunt. Here is all of it, in plain words.

Hunts

Tell Zoopy exactly what you want

A hunt is one saved search that Zoopy keeps running for you. Make it as broad or as picky as you like.

  • Car hunts

    All plans

    Pick one or more makes and models, a year range, a kilometre or mile range and gearbox.

  • iPhone hunts

    All plans

    Choose the models you want and set the most you would pay for each one.

  • Anything else

    All plans

    Type keywords for furniture, tools, bikes, consoles, cameras or anything Marketplace sells.

  • Keyword rules

    All plans

    Use AND, OR and NOT to say what must, may and must not be in a listing.

  • Price range

    All plans

    Set a minimum and a maximum so you only hear about listings you would buy.

Alerts

Alerts you can act on from the lock screen

  • The details that matter

    All plans

  • Reads the whole listing

    All plans

    Filters and hidden words check the description, not just the title.

  • Your sound, your hours

    All plans

    Pick an alert sound or none, and set quiet hours for the night.

  • Open it your way

    All plans

    Choose whether tapping an alert opens the listing in Zoopy or straight in Facebook.

  • Max alerts go first

    Max

    When many alerts go out at once, Max members are sent first.

Filters

Less junk, more of what you want

Marketplace is full of dealers, wrecks and listings that are not what they seem. Zoopy helps you skip them.

  • Hide dealer, salvage and damaged listings

    All plans

    Keep them out of your alerts. They stay in your feed if you want a look.

  • Junk words hidden from the start

    All plans

    New car hunts already hide words like "for parts" and "wrecking". Add your own.

  • Tags from the listing's own words

    Pro and Max

    Dealer, salvage, damaged, urgent, negotiable and firm, shown on each listing.

  • Scam wording warning

    Pro and Max

    A flag when a listing asks for gift cards, a deposit before viewing or a friends-and-family transfer. A hint, not a verdict.

Anywhere

Any place on the map, any radius

Zoopy works wherever Facebook Marketplace runs. Most of our testing so far has been in Australia.

  • Any suburb, town or city

    All plans

    Search by name and pick the spot. No fixed list of cities.

  • Up to 500 km around it

    All plans

    Pick a radius from 25 to 500 km (15 to 300 miles). One hunt covers the circle.

  • Extra areas

    All plans

    Add another area to a hunt, for example your city and your parents' town. Each area uses one hunt.

  • Your units, their currency

    All plans

    Kilometres or miles, and every price in the listing's own currency.

After the alert

Everything you need to decide

Zoopy does not buy or message for you. It gives you what you need to move fast and choose well.

  • Message starters

    All plans

    Ready-to-edit messages to check it is available, make an offer or arrange a pickup. You send them.

  • Price history and similar listings

    All plans

    See if the price has moved, and what else your hunt has caught near that price.

  • Price-drop alerts

    All plans

    Hear when a listing your hunts caught drops by 3% or more. For everything, or only what you saved.

  • Watch what you saved

    Max

    Get told when a saved listing goes pending, sells or comes back on sale.

  • See what sold

    Max

    Filter your catches to the ones that sold or went pending after Zoopy found them.

Numbers are how many hunts each plan can run at that pace. Paces are targets: a busy moment can push a check a little later, and the outer areas of a very wide hunt are checked less often than its centre.

Straight talk

What Zoopy can't do

  • It can't beat Facebook's own delay. New listings stay out of public search for about six to seven minutes after posting, for everyone. Zoopy reaches you after that.
  • It won't catch every listing. Zoopy sees what Facebook shows in public search. Paces are targets, and very wide hunts check their outer areas less often.
  • Filters are not perfect. Tags and hidden words read the listing's own words, so a seller who hides a problem can slip through. Always check the listing and the item yourself.
  • It doesn't message or buy for you. You contact sellers yourself, in Facebook.
  • Price labels are rare for now. Deal labels and value ranges only appear when Zoopy has enough close comparisons, which during the beta is a small share of listings. They use asking prices, not sale prices.
  • It's a phone app. There is no web or desktop version.
