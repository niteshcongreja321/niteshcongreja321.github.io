Facebook Marketplace alerts in Australia

Set up Marketplace alerts anywhere in Australia: suburb and radius tips, AUD prices, rego and PPSR checks for cars, and the alert options that work here.

By the Zoopy team Updated 26 September 2026 7 min read

Quick answer

Facebook Marketplace alerts work the same way across Australia. What changes is where you put the pin and how wide you go: a suburb and 25 to 50 km in the capitals, 100 to 250 km around regional towns. Set a price range in Australian dollars, hide the words you never want, and for cars run a PPSR check before you pay.

Can you get Facebook Marketplace alerts in Australia?

Yes. Marketplace is the same product here as anywhere else, and every way of getting alerts works in Australia: Facebook’s own notifications, and alert apps that run your search on a schedule and push new matches to your phone. Our main guide to Facebook Marketplace alerts covers both, and notifications not working covers what to check when Facebook’s alerts do not arrive.

What is different in Australia is the map. Our cities sprawl, most people live near the coast, and a regional buyer may need to drive a long way for the right car or caravan. Getting the centre point and radius right matters more here than almost anything else.

Where to put the pin, and how wide to go

Marketplace searches around one place and a distance. An alert should do the same, so start by asking where you would actually drive from, and how far you would drive for this item.

Some rules of thumb that follow from the map:

  • Capital cities: 25 to 50 km from your suburb. A pin in your own suburb, not the CBD, keeps distances honest. If you live in Penrith, a 50 km circle around Penrith covers western Sydney far better than one around the city.
  • Big items travel: widen for cars, caravans and boats. Two hours on the road can be worth it for the right HiLux. It is rarely worth it for a bookshelf.
  • Furniture and white goods: keep it tight. Most of it is pick-up only, so you need a trailer or a mate with a ute. Ten or twenty kilometres saves a wasted Saturday.
  • Regional towns: go wide. Around Toowoomba, 100 km takes in Warwick, Dalby and Ipswich. Around Dubbo, Orange is 123 km away, so a 100 km circle misses it.
  • Two cities, two areas. If you would buy in Brisbane or Toowoomba but not in the paddocks between, two smaller circles beat one big one. You see fewer irrelevant listings, and every distance on an alert means something.

Set a minimum price

Some listings carry a placeholder price such as A$1. When we looked at Brisbane’s Vehicles page on 26 September 2026, one of the first listings was priced at AU$1. A sensible minimum price hides most of these.

Prices in Australian dollars

Marketplace shows prices in the listing’s currency, which here is Australian dollars. Set your price range in whole dollars and leave a little room at the top: many private sellers leave room to negotiate, and some say so with “ONO” (or nearest offer). Our guide to checking a Marketplace price helps you judge whether a price is fair before you message.

Buying a car? Know your rego and PPSR checks

If your alert is for a car, the checks after the alert matter more than the alert itself. In short:

  • PPSR search. At the time of writing (September 2026), a search on the Personal Property Securities Register costs $2 online. You need the car’s VIN, not the plate. It tells you if money is owing on the car, and usually whether it is recorded as written off or stolen. Search again on the day you buy.
  • Rego check. Every state has an online registration check. Make sure the plate, VIN and details match the car in front of you.
  • Roadworthy rules differ by state. Queensland needs a safety certificate and Victoria a roadworthy certificate to transfer the rego. The ACT and the NT need an inspection for older light vehicles. NSW, WA, SA and Tasmania do not need one to transfer a registered car.
  • Transfer within 14 days in most states, and pay the transfer fee and stamp duty.
  • Pay safely. Scamwatch warns that scammers sell cars, boats and caravans on marketplaces, and says to inspect big-ticket items in person before paying. When paying by PayID or bank transfer, check the account name matches the seller.

How fast can alerts be in Australia?

Be wary of any promise of alerts “in seconds”. Facebook keeps a new listing out of public search for about six to seven minutes after the seller posts it, for everyone, whatever tool they use. A good alert tool reaches you soon after that point; no search-based tool can show you the listing before it.

Zoopy in Australia

Zoopy is built in Australia and was tested most around Brisbane and south-east Queensland. In our beta tests around Brisbane in September 2026, the typical alert went out about 6½ minutes after the seller posted. It works with Facebook Marketplace in any Australian city or town.

A hunt is a pin and a radius. Type any suburb or town, pick 25, 50, 100, 250 or 500 km, and add extra areas if you need two places (each extra area uses one more hunt). In a wide hunt, the part of the circle nearest your pin is checked at your chosen pace and the outer parts less often, so put the pin where most of what you want is.

Pick a hunt type. Vehicle hunts use makes, models, years, kilometres and gearbox, and start with junk words like “for parts” and “wrecking” hidden. They are built for cars and utes, so hunt a caravan or boat with Something else and keywords instead. iPhone hunts take a model and a buy price for each.

Pick a pace. Turbo on Max targets checks about every 15 seconds; targets of every 3 minutes (Pro and Max), 5 or 10 minutes (all plans). Paces are targets, not promises.

Alerts and filters. Every alert shows the title, the price (plus kilometres, gearbox and fuel for cars) and the suburb and distance. On every plan you can hide dealer, salvage and damaged listings from alerts and get price-drop alerts of 3% or more. Questions people ask

Can you search Facebook Marketplace Australia-wide?

Not directly. Facebook searches around one location and a distance you choose, so covering several cities means several searches or an alert tool that covers a wider area. Our wide-area search guide has the details.

What radius should I use in a capital city?

Start around 25 to 50 km from where you would actually drive from. From Brisbane's CBD, 50 km reaches Ipswich and Caboolture; the Gold Coast needs about 100 km. Widen it only if you are not getting enough matches.

Are Marketplace alert apps priced in Australian dollars?

Zoopy's plans are set in Australian dollars, with a 7-day free trial on monthly plans for new subscribers; see pricing. The app stores show prices in your local currency.

Does Zoopy check the written-off register or the PPSR?

No. Zoopy reads the words in each listing and can hide salvage and damaged listings from your alerts, but it does not search any register. Run a $2 PPSR search on the VIN yourself before you buy a car.

How fast are Marketplace alerts in Australia?

Facebook keeps a new listing out of public search for about six to seven minutes after it is posted, for everyone. In Zoopy's beta tests around Brisbane in September 2026, the typical alert went out about 6½ minutes after the seller posted.
