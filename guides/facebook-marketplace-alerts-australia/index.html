Facebook Marketplace Alerts in Australia: Regional Tips

Zoopy is a global Facebook Marketplace alert app. Australia still has its own quirks — suburb-by-suburb pricing, written-off cars quietly re-listed, and deals that vary wildly between Sydney's inner west and regional towns. Here's how to use Zoopy well on AU Marketplace.

What works well for Australian buying

  • Any suburb + radius. Hunt from where you actually are — search any suburb or town with a radius ring, not a fixed city list. Distance shows on every alert, measured from home or your current location.
  • Write-off and WOVR filtering. Written-off vehicles are common on AU Marketplace and often disclosed only deep in the description. Zoopy reads for it and filters automatically.
  • Local price context. Valuations use comparable listings in your region — a fair price in Perth isn't a fair price in Hobart (or London).

Local hunting patterns that work

  1. Commuter-belt arbitrage: the same iPhone or dresser is consistently cheaper 40–60 km out of the CBD. A wider radius plus distance-on-alert makes the drive/margin call instant.
  2. End of month/lease moves: "moving out", "leaving Sydney" hunts spike with underpriced furniture.
  3. Tradie tools on weekends: upgraded kits get listed Saturday morning; Turbo-speed alerts win them.

Getting started

Create a hunt with your keywords, price band, suburb and radius; pick a check speed (every 15 minutes down to every 15 seconds); turn on lock-screen alerts. Zoopy's plans bill in AUD on monthly, 3-month or 6-month terms — monthly starts with a 7-day free trial.

Regional users: widen the radius and use deals-only alerts. Less volume, but far less competition per deal — regional catches sit longer.

Let Zoopy do the refreshing

Lock-screen alerts in as little as 15 seconds, instant valuations, scam filtering — one good catch pays for the app.
