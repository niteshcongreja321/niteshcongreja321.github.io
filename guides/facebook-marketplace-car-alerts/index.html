Facebook Marketplace Car Alerts: How to Snipe Underpriced Cars

Underpriced private-party cars are the most competitive listings on all of Facebook Marketplace. A clean car listed $2,000 under market gets messages within minutes — from flippers, dealers and buyers running alert tools. If you're checking Marketplace manually, you're not really in the race.

Why car deals vanish fastest

Cars carry the biggest absolute margins on Marketplace, so they attract the most professionalised buyers. Many run monitoring tools already. Meanwhile private sellers often price from memory, not the market — which creates exactly the mispricings everyone is racing to.

What a proper car alert needs

  • Real vehicle filters — make and model (not string-matching "Golf" to golf clubs), year range, kilometres, transmission.
  • Dealer filtering — dealer stock is market-priced; you want private sellers.
  • Damage/write-off filtering — salvage, WOVR and "written off" often hide in the description, not the title. The cheap car that's cheap for a reason isn't a deal.
  • Speed — a check interval measured in seconds, because everyone else's is minutes.

Setting up a car hunt in Zoopy

  1. Choose the vehicle path in the hunt wizard: pick makes (multi-select), then models per make.
  2. Set year range, max kilometres and transmission.
  3. Set your price band and search radius from any suburb.
  4. Pick Turbo (15 s) — for cars, it's the tier that pays for itself.

Every alert arrives with price, distance and a valuation against comparable listings, and Zoopy's filters have already removed dealers, damaged, salvage and written-off stock.

Motivated-seller phrases work for cars too: hunts for "need gone", "moving overseas", "first to see will buy" surface sellers pricing for speed, not value.
