Facebook Marketplace Notifications Not Working? Here's Why — and the Fix

You saved a search, turned on notifications, and… nothing. Or the alert arrived four hours after the listing — long after it sold. Marketplace notifications failing isn't a bug on your phone; it's how Facebook's notification system is designed. Here's what's happening and how to fix it properly.

Why Marketplace notifications arrive late (or never)

  • One queue for everything. Marketplace alerts share a delivery queue with likes, comments, friend requests and group posts. Facebook's ranking system decides what's "worth" pushing, and a niche saved search rarely wins.
  • Batching. To reduce notification fatigue, Facebook bundles alerts and delivers them in waves — fine for social updates, fatal for deals.
  • Silent drops. Many matching listings never generate a notification at all. There is no setting that fixes this.

The checklist worth trying first

  1. Phone Settings → Notifications → Facebook: allow notifications, banners and lock-screen delivery.
  2. In Facebook: Settings → Notifications → Marketplace → enable push.
  3. Disable battery optimisation / low-power restrictions for the Facebook app.
  4. Re-save your search and confirm the bell icon is on.

Do all four and delivery improves from "rarely" to "sometimes, late". That's the ceiling — the delay is server-side, not on your phone.

The actual fix: monitor listings directly

Apps built for this don't wait for Facebook to decide you should know. They check public Marketplace listings on a fixed schedule and push through their own notification pipeline. Zoopy checks as often as every 15 seconds, and its alerts are real push notifications with photo, price, distance and a market valuation — delivered while the listing is still minutes old.

Reality check: no app receives a real-time feed from Facebook. What matters is how often the app checks. Ask any tool one question: "what's your check interval?"
