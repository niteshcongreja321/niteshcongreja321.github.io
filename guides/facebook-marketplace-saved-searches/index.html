Facebook Marketplace saved searches

Where Facebook Marketplace keeps saved searches and alerts, how to edit or delete them on iPhone, Android and desktop, and what to do when one won't go.

By the Zoopy team Updated 26 September 2026 6 min read

Quick answer

A saved search is a Marketplace search Facebook remembers so it can tell you about new matches. Facebook’s help centre doesn’t document where they’re listed, and it varies by app version. On a computer, run the same search again and use its Notify Me button to change or remove the alert. To silence them all, switch off saved-search notifications.

Saved searches, saved items and recent searches: what’s the difference?

Several Marketplace features sound alike, and it’s easy to mix them up when you’re looking for something you saved.

What it is In the iPhone or Android app On a computer
Saved search A search Facebook watches, so it can notify you about new listings Varies by app version (see below) The Notify Me button on that search’s results
Saved item One listing you tapped Save on Menu > Marketplace > Profile > Saved items Marketplace > Buying > Saved

The saved items, followed sellers, recent searches and notification paths come from Facebook’s help centre in September 2026. Facebook is moving some people from Accounts Centre to Meta Account settings, so you may see either name.

Where does Facebook keep your saved searches?

Here is the honest answer: we couldn’t find a Facebook help centre page that says where saved searches are listed (we looked in September 2026). Facebook documents your saved items, your Marketplace inbox and your notification switches, but not a list of saved searches. The layout also changes between app versions, regions and accounts, so two people can see different screens.

Where to look, in order:

  1. On a computer, start from the search itself. Open Marketplace, type the same words, and set the same location and filters. The Notify Me button sits in the left-hand panel under Search results. It belongs to that search, so this is where you change or remove its alert. In How-To Geek’s walkthrough (last updated June 2024), running the search again brought up an Edit Alert option.
  2. In the app, check your Marketplace profile. Tap Menu, then Marketplace, then your profile. That’s where Facebook keeps your saved items, inbox and Marketplace settings, and it’s the first place to look for anything you’ve saved.
  3. In the app, check the search screen. Tap Search in Marketplace and look at what it offers before you type.
  4. If your version shows no list, use a computer. It’s the one place we could confirm the button.

How to edit a saved search

Facebook doesn’t document editing a saved search, so the reliable method is to replace it.

  1. Run the search the way you want it now: new words, a different place or radius, or a new price range.
  2. Save it, or click Notify Me on a computer.
  3. Delete the old search (steps below), so you don’t get two alerts for the same listing.

Change one thing at a time. If you widen the radius and the alerts fill with near-misses, you’ll know which change caused it. If you want two separate areas, keep two searches; our guide to searching a wide area shows how to space them.

How to delete a saved search

On a computer

  1. Open Marketplace and search for the exact words you saved.
  2. Set the same location, distance and filters.
  3. Click Notify Me under Search results and turn the alert off or remove it.

In the iPhone or Android app

  1. Find the saved search: check your Marketplace profile and the search screen.
  2. Open the search’s options and remove it, or turn its notifications off. The label differs between versions.

If you can’t find it anywhere, stop the alerts instead. Tap Menu, Marketplace, your Marketplace profile, Settings, then Notifications, open the saved searches category and turn the toggle grey. The search may still exist, but it will stop notifying you.

What to do when a saved search won’t delete

In Facebook’s own groups, users describe saved searches that won’t delete however they try. If that’s you, work through these in order. The general fixes come from Facebook’s troubleshooting page.

  • Update the Facebook app, close it and restart your phone.
  • Try the other place: if the app won’t let go, delete it from a computer, and the other way round.
  • On a computer, clear your browser’s cache or try a different browser.
  • Log out of Facebook and back in. Facebook suggests this for recent searches that won’t clear, and it’s worth a try here.
  • Turn off saved-search notifications (above), so a stuck search stops pinging you.
  • Report it: in the app, shake your phone and follow the prompts; on a computer, go to Help & Support and choose Report a problem.

How to keep saved searches useful

We found no published limit on how many saved searches you can have. More isn’t better, though. One Facebook group member with more than 50 saved searches asked for a way to see them all on one page, which tells you how quickly they get hard to manage.

  • Be specific. “Weber Q2200” beats “bbq”. Our search tips cover which tricks work in Marketplace search and which don’t.
  • Set a price range. A minimum hides most accessories and parts; a maximum hides what you’d never pay.
  • One area per search. Use the distance you’d actually drive.
  • Prune monthly. Delete searches for things you’ve bought or stopped wanting.
  • Check settings before re-saving. If alerts stop, work through our notifications troubleshooting guide before you delete and re-create everything.

How to clear your recent searches

Recent searches are history, not alerts. Facebook says only you can see them. To clear them:

  • In the app: tap Menu, Settings & privacy, Settings, Accounts Centre, Your information and permissions, then Search history. Choose your account and tap Remove next to a search.
  • On a computer: click the search bar at the top left of Facebook, click Edit, then Clear searches next to Search history.

Facebook notes not everyone can reach this setting in Accounts Centre yet, and that logging out and back in can clear searches that still show.

Every hunt in one list

In Zoopy, a saved search is called a hunt, and every hunt sits in one Hunts tab showing what it looks for, where, and at what pace. From each hunt’s menu you can Edit hunt, Duplicate hunt, Pause hunt (which frees its slot), Mute notifications (it keeps running, and catches still land in your feed) or Delete hunt.

Listings you like go in a separate Saved tab. Price-drop alerts, on every plan, can cover all the listings your hunts caught or only the ones you saved, and fire when a price falls by 3% or more. Zoopy is in private beta on iPhone, with Android next.

Questions people ask

Where are my saved searches on Facebook Marketplace?

Facebook's help centre doesn't say, and the spot varies between app versions. On a computer, run the same search again and use the Notify Me button on the results. In the app, check your Marketplace profile and the search screen.

How do I delete a saved search on Facebook Marketplace?

Run the exact same search again and turn the alert off from its Notify Me or save button. If you can't find the search at all, switch off the saved searches category in Marketplace's notification settings, which stops the alerts.

Why won't my saved search delete?

Other users report the same problem in Facebook's forums. Update the app, restart, try deleting it from a computer instead of the app (or the other way round), log out and back in, then report the problem to Facebook if it still sticks.

Are saved searches the same as saved items?

No. A saved item is one listing you tapped Save on. In the app it lives under Marketplace, Profile, Saved items; on a computer under Marketplace, Buying, Saved. A saved search watches for new listings.

Does clearing my search history remove saved searches?

Treat them as separate. Recent searches are your Facebook search history, which you clear in Accounts Centre. To stop Marketplace alerts, remove the saved search or turn off saved-search notifications.
