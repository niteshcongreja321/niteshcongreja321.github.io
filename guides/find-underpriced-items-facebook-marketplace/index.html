How to Find Underpriced Items on Facebook Marketplace

Underpriced listings appear on Marketplace every day — estates being cleared, people moving in a hurry, sellers who simply don't know what a thing is worth. Finding them isn't about scrolling more; it's about knowing value instantly and hearing about listings the moment they appear.

Why mispricing happens constantly

Private sellers price from memory, from what they paid years ago, or from wanting it gone this weekend. Nobody's running comps on their old Milwaukee kit at 9pm — they type a number that feels fine and hit post. The market's inefficiency is your margin.

The three-part system

1. Know value without thinking

You can't evaluate every listing manually. Zoopy prices each catch against comparable listings automatically and flags it — "$210 under market" right on the alert. Deals become obvious instead of researched.

2. Hunt where mispricing clusters

  • Motivated-seller phrases: "moving sale", "must go", "need gone ASAP", "estate".
  • Generic titles: valuable items listed vaguely — "old table", "tool box", "phone". Specific hunts with broad keywords + your valuation catch these.
  • Late-night and weekday-morning listings: fewer buyers awake, same number of sellers.

3. Hear about it in seconds

An underpriced listing everyone can see is a race; most are decided in minutes. Zoopy checks as often as every 15 seconds and you can restrict alerts to deals only, or to an estimated profit over a threshold — so the phone only buzzes for listings worth sprinting for.

Quiet filter trick: "profit ≥ $100" alerts turn Marketplace from a feed you scroll into a pager that goes off when money appears near you.

Lock-screen alerts in as little as 15 seconds, instant valuations, scam filtering — one good catch pays for the app.
