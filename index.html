How fast, honestly

No app sees a new listing the second it's posted. Here's what really happens.

Facebook keeps a new listing out of public search for about six to seven minutes. That wait is the same for everyone: you, other buyers and every alert app. Zoopy is built to reach you right after it, so you can stop checking and still hear about new listings soon after anyone can see them.

Questions

The things people ask first

Straight answers, including what Zoopy can't do.

All questions
What is Zoopy?

Zoopy is a phone app that watches Facebook Marketplace for you. You make a "hunt" (what you want, your price range, a place and a radius) and Zoopy checks Marketplace around the clock. When a new listing matches, you get a push alert with the photo, price, distance and key details, so you can stop refreshing.

Can I download Zoopy yet?

Not yet. Zoopy is in a private beta on iPhone, and Android comes next. Join the early-access list on this page and we will email you when you can get it.

Do I need to give Zoopy my Facebook login?

No. Zoopy reads public Marketplace listings, the same ones anyone can see without signing in. It never asks for your Facebook password and never posts or messages for you. When you want to contact a seller, you open the listing and message them yourself.

How fast are the alerts?

When someone posts on Marketplace, Facebook keeps the new listing out of public search for about six to seven minutes. That wait is the same for everyone, including every alert app and anyone refreshing by hand. Zoopy is built to reach you right after it. In our beta tests around Brisbane in September 2026, the typical alert went out about 6½ minutes after the seller posted.

Can Zoopy hide dealers, write-offs and damaged listings?

Yes. You can keep dealer, salvage and damaged listings out of your alerts (they stay in your feed if you want to look). New car hunts also start with junk words such as "for parts" and "wrecking" hidden. Zoopy reads the full listing before it alerts you, so these filters check the description, not just the title. No filter is perfect, so always check the listing yourself.

How much does Zoopy cost?

There are three plans: Go (A$36.99 a month, 10 hunts), Pro (A$114.99 a month, 21 hunts) and Max (A$279.99 a month, 24 hunts with Turbo). 3-month and 6-month plans cost less per month. Monthly plans start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Prices are in Australian dollars; the App Store and Google Play show your price in your own currency. See the pricing page.
