FAQ

Questions, answered.

How does Zoopy work?

Create a hunt — keywords, price range, suburb + radius — and Zoopy monitors public Facebook Marketplace listings around the clock, pushing a lock-screen alert with photo, price, distance and an instant valuation the moment a match goes live. Learn more →

Does Zoopy need my Facebook password?

No. Zoopy only monitors public Marketplace listings — you never hand over your Facebook login, and nothing is ever posted or messaged on your behalf. When you want to contact a seller, Zoopy opens the listing in Facebook and you take it from there.

How quickly are alerts sent?

You choose from five check speeds: every 15 minutes (Relaxed), 5 minutes, 60 seconds, 30 seconds, or 15 seconds (Turbo). On hot items, seconds decide who wins. Learn more →

Can I monitor multiple searches at once?

Yes — every hunt runs in parallel with its own keywords, filters, location and check speed. Plans include up to 7 hunts on Go, 16 on Pro and 22 on Max.

Why are Facebook's own notifications late or missing?

Facebook batches saved-search alerts with every other notification type, so they routinely arrive hours late or never. A dedicated monitor like Zoopy checks continuously and alerts in seconds. Learn more →

Can I get alerts for cars?

Yes — Zoopy's vehicle hunts filter by make, model, year range, kilometres and transmission, and automatically hide dealers, damaged, salvage and written-off stock. Learn more →

Is there a free version?

Zoopy is a paid subscription — running thousands of Marketplace checks a day on your behalf isn't free to operate. But every monthly plan starts with a 7-day free trial. Plans start at $36.99 AUD/month, and you can cancel anytime through the App Store or Google Play. One good catch usually pays for months.

Which countries are supported?

Zoopy works anywhere Facebook Marketplace is available — free-text city/area search with radius, multi-country city coverage, and filters for dealers, damaged and salvage stock. Local guides (including Australia) cover market-specific tips.
