The free trial

How the 7 days work

  1. TodayStart a monthly plan. Everything in it unlocks and your hunts start running.
  2. Day 5If notifications are on, Zoopy reminds you the trial is ending.
  3. Day 7Your monthly price starts. Cancel in the App Store or Google Play at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you are not charged.

Billing questions

How much does Zoopy cost?

There are three plans: Go (A$36.99 a month, 10 hunts), Pro (A$114.99 a month, 21 hunts) and Max (A$279.99 a month, 24 hunts with Turbo). 3-month and 6-month plans cost less per month. Monthly plans start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Prices are in Australian dollars; the App Store and Google Play show your price in your own currency. See the pricing page.

Is there a free plan?

No. Running your hunts around the clock costs real money, so Zoopy is a paid app. A free account can look around the whole app with sample data first, and monthly plans start with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed and renewed by the App Store or Google Play. Cancel any time in your store account settings, at least 24 hours before the period ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

What is a hunt, and what counts toward my plan?

A hunt is one saved search: what you want, where, and at what pace. Each plan lets you run a set number at once and at each pace. A paused hunt does not count. Adding an extra area to a hunt uses one more hunt.

Why is there a limit on members?

During the beta we cap the number of paying members so alerts stay fast for everyone. When the cap is full, new sign-ups wait on a list and we let people in as capacity grows.
