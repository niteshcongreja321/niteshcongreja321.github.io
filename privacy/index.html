Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 July 2026

This policy explains what Zoopy ("we", "us") collects when you use the Zoopy mobile app and website, why we collect it, and the choices you have. Zoopy is operated from Australia.

What we collect

  • Account details — your email address and/or phone number, used to create and secure your account (one-time codes are sent to verify a phone number).
  • Your hunts and preferences — the searches you create (keywords, categories, price ranges, locations), alert settings, and shortlisted listings. This is the core of the service: we use it to match listings and send you notifications.
  • Home location — a suburb/city you choose so we can show distances. If you enable "measure from current location", your device location is used on-device to compute distance; it is not stored on our servers.
  • Device push tokens — so notifications can be delivered to your device.
  • Usage and diagnostics — app version, basic event logs (e.g. when a notification was sent or suppressed) so we can support you and keep alerts reliable, and crash reports.
  • Purchase state — your subscription tier and its expiry, synced from the app store you purchased through. We never see or store your card details; payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't show third-party ads.
  • We don't access your Facebook account — Zoopy does not ask for, store, or use any Facebook credentials. Listing data shown in the app comes from publicly visible Marketplace pages.

Service providers

We use a small number of processors to run Zoopy:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (account data, hunts, matches).
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging / Apple Push Notification service — notification delivery.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management (app user id and purchase state).
  • Twilio — delivery of SMS verification codes when you sign in with a phone number.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Danger zone → Delete account, which permanently removes your profile, hunts, matches, shortlist, and push tokens. You can also email us (below) to request deletion.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act (and, where applicable, the GDPR) you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or complain to your local privacy regulator. Contact us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

Zoopy is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the date above; material changes are announced in the app.
